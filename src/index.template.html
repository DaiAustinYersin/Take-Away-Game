    ✧   The strategy behind the game+

    Amy can always win.

    Take 1 coin first, leaving 99. After Beth takes x coins, Amy takes 11 − x. Together, each pair of moves removes 11 coins.

    Amy keeps leaving 99, 88, 77, …, 11, then 0. From a multiple of 11, every legal move lets the other player return to a multiple of 11. So these are losing positions for the player whose turn it is.

    Beth plays optimally, but cannot force a win if Amy follows this strategy.
